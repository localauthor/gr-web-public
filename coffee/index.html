Metal filter (Kone)

A metal filter lets oils and fine particles through, so some body and sediment are normal.

  • Baratza Encore: V60 has worked at 14-19 clicks, AeroPress espresso-style at 14, AeroPress inverted at 26.
  • Hario hand grinder: 3 clicks from the bottom stalled the V60 (over 4:00, muddy). Try 5-6 clicks from the bottom. Settings do not match the Encore's.
  • V60 size 02: Comfortable up to about 35 g of coffee and 450 g of water; these are approximate. For more, brew in two batches or use a 03.
  • Able Kone: Reusable metal filter. Not dialed in yet; grind settings under To try are estimates.
  • Melitta #4: Unbranded cone dripper with a narrow base. Not dialed in yet; check how much coffee it holds before a large batch.
